4b · Author picking at scale. A checkbox per owner (design 4) breaks down once a tab holds changes from dozens of people, and it can never reach someone with no change on the tab. All three variants keep the View panel and the summary strip; only the Author section changes.
  • A reuses the reviewer type-ahead (the account suggestion endpoint already exists). Suggestions are ranked: owners on this tab with counts first, then the rest of Gerrit. Shortcut chips cover the common asks without typing: my own changes, my team's, everyone else's. This is the recommended one.
  • B keeps the browsable list for small tabs and caps it, so it degrades rather than breaks. Two fields in one panel (titles, authors) is the cost.
  • C makes the row the picker. Fastest when the person you want is already on screen, useless when they are not, so it still needs the type-ahead from A. Its owner menu could be added to A as well.
  • Several authors can be picked at once in all three; chips show who, and the summary strip lists them.
