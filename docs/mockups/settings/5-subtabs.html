5 · Sub-tabs. Settings is a tab at the right end of the strip, and its page borrows the board's own structure: a second, lighter tab row for Team, Mergers, Scope, App icon, Menu bar, Window and About. Each sub-tab is one short page, so nothing scrolls far. Changes apply on the spot; instead of a "Saved" mark beside each control, one toast at the bottom names what just happened and offers Undo for a few seconds ("Added dave to the team · Undo"), which is what a Cancel button used to give you.
  • Connection: not a sub-tab with fields but a launcher at the far right of the sub-tab row ("Connection ↗") that opens the sign-in window. That window is the same screen the app shows on first launch, so there is one sign-in form, not two.
  • Compact: both tab rows scroll sideways; the sub-tab row sits directly under the toolbar.
  • Most tab-like of the five, which is what was asked for; the undo toast is the only new widget. Two tab rows on top can look busy on small windows.
