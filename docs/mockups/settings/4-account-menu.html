4 · Account menu and cards. Two different things get two different homes. Who you are is the avatar at the far right of the tab row: its menu names the account and server (the name itself is in the tooltip, so the strip keeps its width) and offers Connection…, Sign out, Check for updates and About. What you prefer is behind the gear, which toggles like a tab and shows a grid of cards. Checkboxes become switches, which read as "takes effect now"; each card shows "Saved" in its corner for a moment after a change.
  • Connection: only in the account menu. It opens a small window with the three fields and Test. About and update checks move there too, so the settings page is only preferences.
  • Compact: the avatar sits on the toolbar row without the name; the cards stack in one column.
  • Clearest split of auth from settings; the account menu is the idiom of most desktop apps. Two icons on the right instead of one.
